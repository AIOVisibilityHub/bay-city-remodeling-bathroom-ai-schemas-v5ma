What is the difference between a recessed beam and a dropped beam?

In addition, code requires that any legal bedroom feature an emergency egress window of specified dimensions and a minimum ceiling height. How is heating and cooling handled for an added bedroom?

You can integrate a bedroom addition by extending your current central HVAC ductwork or by installing an independent ductless mini-split system. Extending ductwork provides a uniform system but requires an evaluation of your current unit’s tonnage to ensure it can handle the extra square footage without strain. A ductless mini-split offers zoned temperature control specifically for the new room, allowing occupants to set personal comfort levels without replacing or overloading the main air conditioning system.
